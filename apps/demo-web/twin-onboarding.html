The creation journey

What must happen before the flame changes.

  1. 01
    Verify your account

    Cirvanna derives the owner from the signed identity session. The browser cannot nominate another user.

  2. 02
    Ask for separate permissions

    Image processing, body measurements and persistent Twin storage are distinct choices. Consent must be resource-bound, revocable and checked before every operation.

  3. 03
    Capture source images

    The production flow must disclose supported angles, image quality, retention and whether any video is accepted before asking you to share media.

  4. 04
    Reconstruct and independently validate

    A separate validator must inspect the actual private mesh bytes, body-surface coverage, scale, topology and privacy properties.

  5. 05
    Persist your owner-bound Twin

    The Twin Registry and private Asset Service must confirm ownership, active consent, private assets and deletion lifecycle before the Twin can appear.

Privacy by purpose

Your permissions stay separate.

Image processingAllows the specifically described image-processing operation. It does not, by itself, authorize long-term image storage.
Body measurementsAllows the specified measurement process. A pose skeleton is not a calibrated measurement or fit result.
Virtual Twin storageAllows an owner-bound persistent Twin and its controlled lifecycle. Withdrawal must revoke access and trigger deletion.
No consent is being recorded on this page. The actual creation workflow must request each permission against a specific Twin resource immediately before the corresponding operation.
