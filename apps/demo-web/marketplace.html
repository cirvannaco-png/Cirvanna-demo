PNELLY · Look It

Search from an outfit reference

Upload a reference image of an outfit you are authorized to use. Look It is separate from Click It: it searches outfit components, does not identify people, and does not publish the uploaded image. Product, merchant, price and offer details remain withheld until CAL authorizes display.

Do not upload images containing children or anyone whose consent you do not have. Adult-worn images are face-masked before visual analysis.

Look It requires an authenticated account and both consent choices. The request consent is short-lived and revoked after processing. Product and price details are not returned before CAL authorization.
