# Marketplace CRFP

**Scope:** CRFP as it applies specifically to Cirvanna marketplace workflows.

This is a distinct scope of the canonical CRFP concept, not a separate acronym and not a substitute for system-wide CRFP. Marketplace-specific requirements, data contracts, lifecycle, and consumers must be documented here once the authoritative marketplace specification is recovered.

## Integration constraints

- Preserve marketplace-specific ownership and requirements.
- Reuse shared system-wide CRFP contracts where the marketplace depends on them.
- Do not duplicate or override system-wide policy or create a competing display arbiter.
- Keep marketplace transport, authorization, and persistence concerns in their existing marketplace service boundaries.
- Do not claim runtime capability until typed contracts, tests, security/privacy review, and deployed integration evidence exist.
